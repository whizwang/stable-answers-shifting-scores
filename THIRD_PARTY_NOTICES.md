## MMLU questions

`questions.csv` includes sampled MMLU questions, answer choices and answer keys, together with this project's review decisions. Question text is also present in the saved conversation logs.

Source: Dan Hendrycks, Collin Burns, Steven Basart, Andy Zou, Mantas Mazeika, Dawn Song and Jacob Steinhardt, *Measuring Massive Multitask Language Understanding* (ICLR 2021).

- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03300

## Generated materials and replies

`arguments.csv`, `essays.csv` and `essays_v1.csv` contain model-generated evaluation materials. Response files contain the prompts and model outputs collected for the study. The paper describes which models generated, reviewed and evaluated them. Some materials intentionally contain misleading arguments or errors.
